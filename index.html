Air freight is billed using the higher of actual weight or volumetric weight.

How the calculator works

Step 1: Actual weight must be in kilograms. If weight is in pounds, divide by 2.20462.

Step 2: Volumetric weight (kg) = (Length × Width × Height in inches) ÷ 366.

Step 3: Chargeable weight is the higher of actual weight or volumetric weight.

Step 4: Air cost = Chargeable weight × $15.

A minimum charge of $15 applies plus $5 customs clearance.
